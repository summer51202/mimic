# Task Board Icon Variants Design

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Phase:** Page Visual Optimization Phase 1 — Icons calibration  
**Status:** Approved visual direction; awaiting written-spec confirmation before generation

## Goal

Create three review-only replacements for the primary-navigation Tasks icon. Each candidate must raise the current quest board's material, silhouette, and paper-detail fidelity to the level of the approved campfire, travel journal, warrior/mage handshake, and repaired gear icons.

## Shared visual contract

- Preserve the meaning of a fantasy quest or task notice board.
- Match the approved Mimiku high-density hand-drawn pixel language: stepped dark-navy outlines, clustered pixels, limited warm color ramps, restrained highlights, and slight handmade asymmetry.
- Use a transparent background with no scenic base, words, letters, numbers, UI chrome, watermark, or drop shadow outside the object.
- Keep a compact, centered, near-square silhouette suitable for a future 192 px master and a separately optimized 48 × 48 export.
- Maintain the same apparent weight, density, outline strength, and warm wood/brass palette as the other four approved primary-navigation icons.
- Do not alter or regenerate the other four primary-navigation icons.

## Candidate directions

### A — Adventurer Contract Board (recommended)

A thick rectangular timber board with visible layered planks, substantial brass corner protectors, three overlapping parchment contracts, varied pins, and one small red wax seal. This remains closest to the current Tasks meaning while adding the missing material depth and focal detail.

### B — Guild Quest Wall

A slightly arched timber sign with a carved upper rail, rope or leather hanging detail, layered parchment notices, and refined brass fasteners. It should feel more ceremonial than A without resembling the Adventurer Guild handshake icon.

### C — Expedition Planning Board

A shield-like timber frame containing a map fragment, a pinned contract, a small route marker, and one restrained brass badge. It carries the strongest adventure character while remaining immediately readable as Tasks rather than Settings or Maps.

## Review output

- Generate one transparent PNG for each candidate using the built-in ImageGen workflow.
- Preserve all three candidates as versioned review assets under the Phase 1 review directory.
- Present A, B, and C together at equal scale with short labels outside the raster pixels.
- Do not replace the approved navigation source, recomposite the main calibration, promote runtime assets, or change the Icons family decision until the user selects a candidate.

## Acceptance checks

- All three candidates are visibly more detailed than the current task board.
- Each remains legible as a task/quest board at navigation-icon scale.
- None contains malformed paper edges, broken transparency, stray fragments, illegible pseudo-text, or visual elements borrowed from adjacent navigation icons.
- The three choices differ in silhouette and information hierarchy, not merely color.
