# Ancient treasure chest — actual ImageGen requests

Built-in imagegen; transparent background. No third-party survey artwork was supplied to ImageGen. Attempt 01 was rejected as too close to the previous generic design. Attempt 02 is a new generation from warrior references. Attempts 03 and 04 edit the preceding source. Selected: attempt-04.png, copied unchanged to family-source.png.

## Attempt 01

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE coordinated animation sprite sheet, 4 columns by 2 rows, of a SINGLE ordinary treasure chest found in an ancient treasure room and repaired by an adventuring party. Genuine transparent background, no scenery. This is a carefully drawn 2D RPG PIXEL SPRITE, NOT a rendered product icon or painted illustration with a pixel filter.

Reference roles: Image 1 warrior master is the primary drawing-style reference. Image 2 actual seated warrior sprite is the exact pixel-density and crisp-material reference. Image 3 room is ONLY perspective, relative prop size and warm firelight context: change nothing in this room and DO NOT reproduce the room, title, phone or characters in the output. Image 4 rejected chest sheet is ONLY the 4x2 layout, facing, body footprint and pose sequence reference; REPLACE ITS visual design. Do not repeat its huge square padlock, evenly spaced shiny silver straps, silver patch or product-render highlights.

Design: a restrained, old, valuable handcrafted chest, once carefully made and now repaired for continued use. Rounded arched wooden lid; left end panel visible and broad front visible, matching room perspective. Dark warm walnut / muted reddish-brown timber, a few broad planks, quiet large planes. Heavy but modest dark warm iron bands with gently narrowed ends and purposeful angular joints, NOT golden ornament. One long compact mechanical hasp with a modest keyhole, connected to the lid and clear of the body when opening; NOT a large square plate and NOT a face. Original workmanship creates character through joinery, silhouette and the rhythm of the two main structural bands, NOT decorative symbols or jewels. One unobtrusive repaired lower corner in similar warm dark iron with slightly different fastening; visually integrated, NOT bright silver. Slight polish only at touched latch and rim, a few selective worn edge notches. Well maintained and solid, not rotten/rusty, not cartoony toy.

Pixel grammar: deliberately placed fine stepped contours, brown-black selective outlines, compact coherent clusters like the warrior's armor and shield wood, 2-3 principal shades per material, small broken warm highlights. Draw as low-resolution pixel art from the outset, shown enlarged with hard square pixel edges. No smooth gradients, airbrushing, antialiasing, blur, 3D bevels, glossy mobile-game gold, dense scratches, noise, dithering fields, irregular AI ornament. Richness comes from clear construction not texture clutter.

Eight same-sized cells in row-major order: 1 closed and latched; 2 closed lid, hasp halfway releasing; 3 closed lid, hasp fully lifted clear; 4 lid cracked just a narrow slit; 5 half open; 6 wider open; 7 nearly fully open; 8 fully open at rest. Same exact lower body, bands, feet, repair and perspective in all eight cells. Never rescale/recenter a chest between cells. Same horizontal center and same feet baseline; leave identical generous headroom for lids and transparent margins. Use an exact regular 4x2 lattice, no text or separator lines. Body width about 74% of cell width; closed visible height about 63% of cell height; baseline at 92% cell height. Closed width/height ratio about78/66. Open lid stays within cell.

Mechanics: rear hinge remains connected, lid rotates rather than sliding upward or floating; hasp releases BEFORE lid lifts and can settle after lid closes. Lower body is rigid, no breathing or perspective drift. Inside is dark timber, irregular small overlapping gold coin heaps mostly behind rim, a restrained small blue-teal gem cluster as secondary accent; coin details are tiny pixel clusters, not enormous glowing yellow candy. Entire contents stay within chest, lit only by reflected room firelight, no beams, halo or emitted light. No added props, characters, words, digits, currency symbols, eyes, mouth, teeth, UI, ground plane, cast-shadow platform. Transparent pixels around every state.
```

No room, title, characters, ground, shadow platform, text, symbols, particles, extra props. GENUINE TRANSPARENT background.
