# Landing title inlay — subdued tone v10

Date: 2026-10-07
Mode: built-in ImageGen, three independent lighting-weather edits.
Status: concept-only, awaiting user visual choice. Original v09 files and the selected layered review remain unchanged.

## Change and review boundary

Only the intended title lighting is revised: darker walnut wood, subdued brass, warm grey eye/teeth highlights, preserving readable material steps. Compare title illumination with the neighboring rock, not the bright campfire foreground. A/B/C retain their previous junction designs as conceptual alternatives.

Self-review: all three outputs have been visually inspected. A also dims the foreground Mimiku unintentionally; B has small foreground tone drift. C preserves its brighter companion more closely. These full-scene PNGs are not byte-preserving replacements and must not be promoted wholesale. Only the chosen title tone/junction treatment is eligible for later integration; preserve existing approved character layers, wordmark design, live text and scene geometry. No production code, runtime asset or selected review changes.

## Exact submitted prompts

### A

Input: samples/landing-war-room/inlay-options-v09/A.png

```text
Use case: lighting-weather.
Asset type: preview-only raster concept for a Landing pixel RPG room.
Input image 1: edit target, the complete existing inlay concept.
Primary request: Make ONLY the wooden MIMIC title's brightness match the surrounding warm dim rock wall more closely. The current title reads as a brighter pasted-on object; reduce its overall illumination and especially its pale gold/brass and white eye/teeth highlights. Title should seem under the SAME weak ambient warm light as the wall, not a separately illuminated sign.
Tone: noticeably subdued medium-dark warm walnut, muted brown-gold brass, warm grey/off-white eyes and teeth rather than white. Retain recognizable wood, original red heart and eyes details, all letter forms. Do not convert wood into stone or recolor the title uniformly brown. Preserve dark outlines and material color steps. No emissive highlights, no glow, no independent spotlight, no translucency. Keep the title readable by silhouette while campfire/party remain more prominent.
Text (verbatim): "MIMIC", M-I-M-I-C.
Strict edit boundaries: change only color/lightness INSIDE the existing wooden letter and brass/eye/heart pixels. Preserve exactly the existing stone fissures, inset grooves, rock lips and contact shadows. Preserve title size, position, spacing and all its design details. Preserve the entire scene outside the wooden title: all characters, Mimiku, torch/fire, rocks, props, bats, login and bottom Chinese dialogue/CTA, camera framing and pixel-cluster granularity. No additions, no crop, no new text or labels. No smooth airbrushing or noise.
Output: one complete scene retaining input framing, not a collage. This is only a localized lower-brightness revision.
Variant A: retain this exact variant's existing wall junction unchanged; only dim its MIMIC wood/brass.
```
